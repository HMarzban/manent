# Changelog

What changed in Manent, newest first, written for a reader who did not see the work. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). No release has been made yet, so every entry is under Unreleased. Each entry begins with the day it reached `main`.

## [Unreleased]

- 2026-11-26 — Ghost Follow viewport outline indicator and return-to-caret strip in the Scenes panel (`@manent/editor`, `@manent/ui`, `apps/web`, `docs`). Passively tracks the scene or knot currently in the editor viewport's reading zone on animation frames (using O(log n) visible-block lookup that skips hidden omitted blocks, with zero document walks) and publishes it over an isolated `publishViewed` channel on the outline store. Marks the scrolled-to scene in both List and Cards views with a subtle dashed ghost rail (`data-viewed="true"`, with high-contrast and forced-colours parity) distinct from the solid caret highlight, gently auto-scrolls the Scenes list and card grid to keep the viewed scene in sight while pausing automatically on hover, focus, drag or manual sidebar scroll, and displays a compact `Viewing …` / `Back to …` strip beneath the Scenes toolbar whenever the viewport moves away from the caret's scene. Documented across `docs/development/design-system.md`, `docs/guide/writing.md`, `docs/architecture/overview.md`, `docs/memory/lessons.md` and `README.md`.
