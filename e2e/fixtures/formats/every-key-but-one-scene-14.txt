INT. SEAMEN'S INSTITUTE - HALL - NIGHT

The upright stands open under the honours boards, its hammers bare. Mira has the tuning hammer on a pin in the top octave. Lily waits by the door, her coat still on.

The hall lights are on a timer. Halfway through the unison they click, and go out.

Dark. Only the green of the exit sign over the door. Mira does not stop. She keeps the tuning hammer on the pin and lays her other hand flat on the frame.

LILY
Do you want me to find the switch?

MIRA
No. Play me a C. The top one.

Lily feels her way along the wall to the keyboard and plays it.

Mira waits, her palm on the wood.

MIRA
Again.

Lily plays it again. Mira turns the pin a hair.

MIRA
Again.

The lights come back on. Mira is looking at her hand, not at the piano.
