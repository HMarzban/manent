# Keyboard shortcuts

The default keys. "Ctrl" is ⌘ on a Mac unless a row says otherwise. Every command, with or without a key, is in the command palette (Ctrl+K), and **Preferences → Keyboard** (Ctrl+/) lists them all and lets you change any key that is not fixed.

| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Commands (palette) | Ctrl+K                                                 |
| Go to              | Ctrl+G                                                 |

| Redo               | ⌘⇧Z on a Mac; Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z elsewhere (fixed) |

**Zoom.** In a browser, Ctrl+=, Ctrl+- and Ctrl+0 are left to the browser's own zoom, and the page zooms from **View ▾ → Zoom**. In the desktop app they are Zoom in, Zoom out and Zoom: 100 %.

## Command palette

Open the command palette with **Ctrl+K** (⌘K on a Mac) from anywhere. Before you type, it adapts to where you are — showing a quiet context caption beside the filter strip (`Library`, `Screenplay · Scene 4`, `Locked · Pink`, `Rundown`, `Version history` and so on) and concise contextual sections bounded to stay easy to scan:

- **Suggested here** surfaces runnable actions for the open screen, document kind, caret position, selection or production lock.
- **Jump to** surfaces up to three direct jumps when relevant: your recent **Continue** scripts from the Library, the current speaker's next line while editing dialogue (when they speak more than once), and active collaborators in the open script.
- **Settings here** surfaces live settings for your current context; pressing **Enter** or clicking a switch flips it in place without closing the palette.
- **Last used** keeps your most-used recent commands on this device (deduplicated against the sections above).
- **Menus** lists the full menu groups; press **Enter** or **→** on a menu to browse inside it, and **←** or **Backspace** on an empty field to return.

Press **Tab** (or **Shift+Tab**) to filter by **All**, **Commands**, **Settings**, **Go to** or **Scripts**, or type a leading prefix shortcut (`>` for Commands, `,` for Settings, `#` for Go to, `/` for Scripts). Search matches word starts, apostrophe-free forms (`contd` finds `(CONT'D)`), multi-word acronyms (`fm` finds `Focus mode`) and common synonyms (`zen`, `sidebar`, `scene numbers`, `checks`, `board`).

You can also jump straight to panels, settings panes and numeric figures from the palette:

- **Direct panel and screen jumps:** type **Scenes** (**Chapters** in a manuscript, **Knots** in Ink), **Cuts**, **Details**, **Comments**, **Checks** (opens and expands pre-send Checks in Details), **Board** (on a series episode, keeping the current episode selected), or **Preferences: Writing**, **Preferences: Appearance**, **Preferences: Account** and **Preferences: This browser** (**Preferences: This device** in the desktop app).
- **Inline numeric settings:** run **Lines per page**, **Read rate** or **Target length** to enter a figure directly inside the palette with immediate validation, Undo, and empty-submission reset on **Lines per page** and **Target length**. Locked scripts and non-owner project roles explain clearly why a figure cannot change.

**Escape** closes the palette in one press and returns focus to where you were.

## Find and Go to

**Find** (Ctrl+F) opens the Find bar above the page, holding the selected text if there is any. It looks as you type and counts the matches ("3 of 17"). Enter goes to the next match and Shift+Enter to the previous one; Escape closes the bar. **Replace** opens a second field with **Replace** and **Replace all**; it is there only for people who can edit the script.

**Go to** (Ctrl+G) opens the command palette on places: the script's scenes ("Sc. 12 · INT. LIGHTHOUSE - NIGHT"), its pages, a character's next line, your scripts and projects, and the people in the script. Type to narrow the list, and Enter goes there.

## Commands with no key

General, End of Act, Emote, Camera, Engine Direction, Strikethrough, All-caps, the text colours and highlights, the alignments, Space before, Ruler, the other zoom commands, Where am I?, Script settings, Rename, Export…, Import…, New script, New project, Library, Sync centre, Switch person, Tour, the version history commands (Changes since, Restore, Restore this scene, Who wrote this? and the rest) and the other production commands. Find them in the palette, or give them a key in Preferences → Keyboard.
