Two windows, one script, and no signal

A real recording, 21 November 2026. One window loses its network and keeps writing. 30 seconds, no sound.

What the recording shows
  1. Two windows are open on The Long Night, both signed in as Sam Okafor. Both read Saved · Synced.
  2. Window 1 types. Tab makes the character name MARA, Enter starts her dialogue, and every letter appears in window 2 as it is typed.
  3. Window 2 types a line of action, and it appears in window 1.
  4. Window 2's network is cut. It keeps writing, and its status reads Offline, 4 waiting. Window 1 does not have those lines yet.
  5. The network comes back. Window 2 reads Syncing…, then Synced, and window 1 now holds every line.
