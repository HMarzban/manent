Two windows, one script, and no signal

A real recording, 27 November 2026. Dana Reyes and Sam Okafor write together, one loses network and catches up, followed by locked Blue revisions and a live newsroom rundown. 45 seconds, no sound.

What the recording shows
  1. Two windows are open on 204 The Ferry: Dana Reyes on the left and Sam Okafor on the right. Both read Saved · Synced.
  2. Dana writes a line of action and dialogue for CALLUM, then selects a phrase and leaves a comment for Sam. Every letter and the comment badge appear live in Sam's window.
  3. Sam's network is cut. He keeps writing on his own device, and his status reads Saved · Offline, 3 waiting. Dana does not have those lines yet.
  4. Sam's network comes back. His status returns to Saved · Synced, and Dana's window holds every line he wrote offline.
  5. On The Long Night, locked pages show Blue revision marks and Ghost Follow tracks the scene on screen with Back to Sc. 1.
  6. On Lannick One News, the rundown tracks Air, Target and Under times live, holding edits inside the air window until Send to prompter is chosen.
