Design system

Calm craft that stays.

Every surface, logo proportion, studio palette and transition in Manent follows one rule: protect the writer's eyes over long sessions and never move a single line break on the page.

Brand identity

Rooted in scripta manent — bound archival sheets anchored by a live vertical caret and revision spine. Inside the app, the pillar adapts to your studio palette; external assets stay in canonical Studio Blue.

Bound "M" Monogram

Matched 8.5 px stems with a 4 px architectural slit separating the folded ink sheet from the live focus bar. Sharp from 16 px favicons to desktop Docks and 1-bit print stamps.

Monogram Ligature

Standalone typographic wordmark: a dedicated 6.8 px optical M with a tight 2.6 px inner slit matched to Inter SemiBold so the accent bar locks unmistakably into the letterform.

Long-session eye-health standard

Every palette ships in both Light and Dark schemes and obeys the same mathematical visual ergonomics floor.

Live contrast & print-paper proof

Computed from the active studio palette's live CSS tokens.

47. EXT. FERRY SLIP — DAY
Margins, line breaks and page counts stay invariant across all 24 themes; print and PDF always render on archival white paper.

Zero dark halation

Dark schemes never pair pure #000000 grounds with pure #ffffff text, preventing astigmatism blooming during evening drafts.

Low evening blue scatter

Dark script surfaces cap short-wavelength blue output while Light themes use warm archival substrates rather than harsh blue-white glare.

Pagination & print invariance

Themes alter colour and subtle emblems only. Margins, line breaks and page counts stay identical everywhere, and print or PDF always uses archival white paper.

System accessibility wins

Every palette yields unconditionally to prefers-contrast: more, Windows forced-colors: active and prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.

Architecture rules & icon credits

All colour literals live inside one stylesheet (packages/ui/src/theme.css), every overlay surface (including the smart ⌘K Command Palette, menus and dialogs) adapts automatically across all 24 studio themes and system contrast/motion modes, personal theme choices never change a collaborator's screen, and all 24 collector Hidden Gem emblems come from game-icons.net under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).
